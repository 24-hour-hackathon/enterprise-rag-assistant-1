CORPORATE IT AND INFORMATION SECURITY POLICY

Mandatory Password Policy
All employee passwords must be changed every 90 days without exception.
Passwords must be at least 12 characters in length and include uppercase, lowercase, numbers, and special symbols.

Authentication and Workstations
Multi-factor authentication (MFA) is mandatory on all corporate workstations and VPN access.
Employees must lock their computer screens when leaving their workstations unattended.
