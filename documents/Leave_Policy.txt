ENTERPRISE LEAVE POLICY AND GUIDELINES

Casual Leave Entitlement
Employees are entitled to 12 days of casual leave per calendar year.
Casual leave is intended for urgent personal matters and short unexpected absences.

Annual Leave Entitlement
Employees are entitled to 18 days of annual leave per calendar year.

Leave Application Rules
Leave requests should normally be submitted at least 3 working days before the intended leave date.
Unused casual leave days lapse at the end of each calendar year.
