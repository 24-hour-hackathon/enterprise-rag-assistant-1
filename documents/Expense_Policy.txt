CORPORATE EXPENSE AND TRAVEL REIMBURSEMENT POLICY

Dinner and Meal Reimbursement Policy
Dinner expense reimbursement requires an itemized receipt submitted within policy guidelines.
Daily meal allowance is capped at 75 USD per day during business travel.

Travel Expense Rules
All travel expenses must be submitted with valid receipts within 30 days of completion of business travel.
Reimbursement requests without proper receipts or approval will not be processed.
